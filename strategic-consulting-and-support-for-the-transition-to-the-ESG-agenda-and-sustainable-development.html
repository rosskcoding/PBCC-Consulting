Services
Strategic Consulting and Support
for Transitioning to ESG and
Sustainability Agendas
Our experts provide comprehensive consulting services to support effective strategic planning and your company’s transition to ESG.

  • Comprehensive ESG Reporting Workshops
    Our workshops provide an in-depth study of key standards such as GRI, TCFD, SASB, CSRD/ESRS, ISBB/IFRS, CoP/SDG, and the main aspects of ESG reporting. We ensure that, upon completion, you will be equipped to create reports with confidence, clarity, and alignment with best industry practices.
  • GRI Standards Certification Training
    Whether you are an experienced sustainability professional or a newcomer, our certified GRI Standards training will enhance the quality of your ESG reporting, helping you understand and coordinate the reporting process in line with GRI requirements. Upon completion, you will receive a globally recognised GRI certificate that validates your expertise and credentials in sustainability.
  • Reporting under ESRS (CSRD), SFDR, and the EU Taxonomy
    Through our courses on CSRD, the EU Taxonomy, ESRS, and SFDR, you will gain a detailed understanding of European regulatory requirements. We prepare you to meet the new sustainability reporting standards within the EU framework.
  • Workshops on IFRS S1 and S2 Reporting Standards
    Following the release of the new IFRS S1 and S2 standards by the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB), our workshops offer in-depth knowledge and practical skills to successfully navigate this critical shift in sustainability reporting on global financial markets.
