Decarbonisation strategy

Approach and tools:


Analysis of the company’s carbon profile:
  • calculation of direct and indirect emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3)
  • identification of key emission sources and carbon-intensive processes
  • benchmarking against industry emission factors and the targets of leading companies
Formulation of emission reduction targets:
  • definition of short-, medium- and long-term targets
  • alignment with international initiatives (SBTi, Net Zero, Paris Agreement)
  • linkage of targets with national decarbonisation strategies and climate policy
Development of a decarbonisation roadmap:
  • implementation of energy-efficient technologies and green energy
  • optimisation of production processes and modernisation of equipment
  • decarbonisation of the supply chain and engagement with suppliers
  • application of circular economy principles
  • waste and resource management
Financial modelling and investment decisions:
  • scenario modelling of decarbonisation costs and benefits
  • analysis of available financing sources (green bonds, ESG loans)
  • integration of decarbonisation projects into CAPEX and investment strategy
Monitoring and management:
  • establishment of a KPI system and regular monitoring of progress
  • preparation for external audits and data verification
  • disclosure of information in line with international standards  (ISSB/IFRS S2, ESRS E1, CDP)

Results:


  • A detailed decarbonisation roadmap with clear targets and KPIs.
  • Reduction of the company’s carbon footprint and preparation for Net Zero.
  • Lower operating costs through energy efficiency and process optimisation.
  • Readiness for international and national requirements (CBAM, climate taxonomies, mandatory disclosures).
  • Increased investment attractiveness and access to green financing.
  • Strengthened corporate reputation as a responsible contributor to the global climate agenda.
