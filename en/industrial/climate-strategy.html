Climate strategy

Approach and tools:


Analysis of the company’s current carbon profile:
  • assessment of direct and indirect emissions (Scope 1, 2 and 3)
  • identification of carbon-intensive processes and supply chains
  • comparison with industry emission factors and best practices
Diagnosis of climate risks and opportunities:
  • analysis of physical risks (extreme weather events, resource intensity)
  • analysis of transition risks (regulatory changes, CBAM, national decarbonisation targets)
  • identification of opportunities (energy efficiency, green products, access to financing)
Scenario modelling and strategic planning:
  • scenario analysis in line with TCFD and ISSB/IFRS S2
  • financial assessment of climate scenario impacts on the business
  • determination of high-vulnerability areas and strategic priorities
Development of a climate strategy and roadmap:
  • setting emission reduction targets (including Net Zero)
  • development of an initiative package: energy efficiency, transition to renewable sources, supply chain decarbonisation
  • integration of climate-related KPIs into the corporate management system
Integration and disclosure:
  • preparation of disclosures in reporting (TCFD, ISSB/IFRS S2, ESRS E1)
  • embedding climate objectives into corporate strategy and investment programmes
  • development of monitoring mechanisms and regular updates to the climate strategy

Results:


  • A comprehensive climate strategy and roadmap.
  • Readiness for international and national climate requirements (including CBAM and Net Zero targets).
  • Financial models and scenario forecasts incorporating climate risks.
  • Reduction of carbon footprint and costs through energy efficiency and supply chain optimisation.
  • Enhanced investment attractiveness through transparency and alignment with international standards (TCFD, ISSB, ESRS).
  • Increased trust from investors, regulators and key partners.
