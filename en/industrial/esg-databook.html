Services
ESG Databook

Approach and tools:


Consolidation of the company’s non-financial data
  • into a single structure (ESG Databook in Excel/BI system format).
Systematisation of indicators across the E–S–G pillars:
  • environmental, social and governance metrics, including data on emissions, resources, personnel and corporate governance.
Definition of key ESG indicators and KPIs,
  • aligned with international standards (GRI, SASB, TCFD, IFRS S1/S2, ESRS, SDGs).
Documentation of calculation methodologies and data sources,
  • with clear allocation of responsibilities across departments, enabling audit and verification.
Preparation of analytical materials
  • based on the database for internal management and external communications (investors, regulators, rating agencies).

Results:


  • A strategic management tool: the leadership gains a single source of up-to-date ESG data for monitoring progress and assessing business resilience.
  • Effective communication with external stakeholders: a ready-to-use database for preparing annual, integrated and sustainability reports.
  • Facilitated interaction with ESG rating agencies: the availability of structured data accelerates participation in international ratings and indices.
  • Increased investment attractiveness: transparency and comparability of ESG indicators enhance trust from the financial community and partners.
  • Flexibility and scalability: the ESG Databook can be easily updated and adapted to new regulatory requirements and standards.
