Services
Assessment of climate risks
and opportunities

Approach and tools:


Identification of relevant financial risks
  • identification of physical risks (extreme weather events, resource intensity) and transition risks (regulation, carbon taxes, CBAM)
Development of calculation methodology
  • design of an approach for quantitative and qualitative assessment of risk impacts on financial position, cash flows and company value
  • consideration of short-, medium- and long-term horizons
Data collection and verification
  • work with the company’s financial model and its adaptation to ESG indicators and climate projections
  • verification of data completeness and accuracy
Scenario modelling
  • scenario analysis in line with TCFD and ISSB/IFRS S2
  • assessment of financial impacts of different combinations of climate (physical and transition) and environmental scenarios
  • identification of high-vulnerability areas and determination of strategic opportunities
Preparation of disclosures
  • drafting of texts for inclusion in non-financial and/or financial reporting
  • ensuring compliance with international standards (ISSB, SASB, TCFD, ESRS)
Presentation of results
  • final session explaining the approach, findings and scenarios
  • practical recommendations for integrating results into strategy, risk management and financial planning

Results:


  • Financial models (Excel) integrating ESG factors and climate risks.
  • Draft disclosures (Word) for inclusion in reporting in line with ISSB/SASB/TCFD.
  • Final management presentation with key findings and recommendations.
  • Company readiness to meet investor and regulatory requirements on climate disclosures.
  • Tools to reduce business vulnerability and identify new opportunities (energy efficiency, green financing).
