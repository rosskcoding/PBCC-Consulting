Services
Preparation of ESG reporting

The approach we propose enables:


  • Ensuring reliable and transparent disclosure of the company’s performance, strategy and contribution to sustainable development.
  • Strengthening the link between financial and non-financial indicators and the long-term corporate strategy, reinforcing accountability for societal well-being and future generations.
  • Systematically identifying and managing ESG risks, thereby enhancing the company’s resilience and competitiveness in the global arena.
  • Improving the reporting system and communication with stakeholders, adapting it to current challenges and the expectations of the international community.

Scope of Services:


  • Development of a work schedule for report preparation and organisational/administrative documentation to ensure effective project management.
  • Preparation of recommendations on report content, taking into account:
    • international standards (GRI, TCFD, SASB, ESRS, IFRS S1/S2)
    • methodologies and requirements of rating agencies (MSCI, S&P Global CSA, CDP, Sustainalytics, Moody’s, ESG Fitch)
    • stock exchange and regulatory requirements
  • Preparation of information requests related to the report and coordination of work with the company’s departments.
  • Consolidation of information, preparation of analytical materials and drafting of the report.
  • Professional design and layout of the report in line with international practice.
  • Preparation of an official translation of the report for an international audience.

Results:


  • A fully prepared ESG, annual or integrated report, compliant with international standards and aligned with rating agency expectations.
  • Strengthened trust of investors and stakeholders through transparency and data comparability.
  • Enhanced company competitiveness and improved positioning in ESG ratings.
