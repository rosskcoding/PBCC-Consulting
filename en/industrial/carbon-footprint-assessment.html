Services
Carbon footprint assessment

Approach and tools:


Data collection and verification:
  • collection of information on direct and indirect emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3)
  • verification of data completeness and accuracy, source validation
Emission calculation in line with the GHG Protocol:
  • application of industry and regional emission factors
  • use of the S&P Global Trucost database (covering 4.5 million suppliers)
  • automated calculation of Scope 3 (categories 1 and 2) based on supplier disclosures or  modelled data
Supply chain and indirect emissions analysis:
  • identification of suppliers with the greatest contribution to the carbon footprint
  • estimation of emissions in the absence of data using average emission factors (Trucost)
  • application of Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) methods for Scope 3 categories (3–14)
Client tools:
  • carbon footprint calculator (CIQ Pro Environmental Template) for independent data updates
  • recommendations on organising information collection processes within the company
Benchmarking:
  • comparison of the company’s carbon profile with industry and regional emission factors
Forecasting and scenario modelling:
  • development of carbon footprint scenarios in the short-, medium- and long-term
  • comparison with national and corporate decarbonisation targets
  • determination of target benchmarks and a Net Zero trajectory

Results:


  • A detailed carbon footprint report: absolute emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3) and intensities (per unit of revenue and production).
  • Identification of key emission sources and carbon-intensive processes.
  • Practical recommendations for emission reduction: from immediate measures (energy efficiency, supply chain optimisation) to long-term decarbonisation strategies.
  • Readiness for disclosure to investors and regulators in line with international requirements (GHG Protocol, ISSB/IFRS S2, ESRS).
  • Preparation of the company for participation in ESG ratings, sustainability indices and compliance with CBAM requirements.
