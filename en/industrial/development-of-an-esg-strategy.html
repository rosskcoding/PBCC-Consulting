Services
Development of ESG strategy

Approach and tools:


Comprehensive diagnosis of current ESG practices:
  • analysis of corporate policies, procedures and strategies
  • assessment of the completeness and quality of non-financial disclosures
  • audit of the corporate website and stakeholder communication channels
  • targeted interviews and surveys of management and key employees
Benchmarking and international comparison:
  • comparison with international standards (GRI, SASB, TCFD, IFRS S1/S2, ESRS, SDGs, UN Global Compact)
  • alignment with the practices of peer companies and industry leaders, including results of assessments by international ESG agencies
Compliance with rating agency and regulatory requirements:
  • CSA S&P Global, MSCI ESG Ratings, Sustainalytics
  • consideration of national regulatory initiatives and stock exchange requirements (including decarbonisation, taxonomies, CBAM and others)
Identification of material ESG topics and key risks:
  • prioritisation of topics through stakeholder expectation analysis
  • consideration of climate, operational, regulatory and reputational risks
Development of an ESG strategy and transformation roadmap:
  • formulation of strategic goals and priorities
  • design of initiatives, including quick wins and long-term steps
  • establishment of a KPI system for management and functional units
Integration of ESG into business processes and corporate strategy:
  • embedding ESG objectives into corporate goals
  • recommendations on ESG disclosure, aligned with international standards and investor expectations

Results:


  • A completed ESG strategy and ESG transformation roadmap with clear KPIs.
  • Systematic management of ESG risks and enhanced business resilience.
  • Readiness for international and national requirements (including CBAM and sectoral taxonomies).
  • Improved ESG ratings, reduced cost of capital and increased investment attractiveness.
  • Strengthened trust among investors, regulators, employees and local communities.
