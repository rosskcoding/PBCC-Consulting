Services
Preparation and Support for Companies
in Interaction with International
Sustainability/ESG Rating Agencies
This service is designed to improve your company’s ESG ratings, ensuring a competitive position in the market.

  • Preparation for ESG Ratings
    We thoroughly prepare your company for participation in leading ESG ratings, such as CSA S&P Global ESG Scores, Sustainalytics, Moody’s, and MSCI. Our analysts conduct a comprehensive assessment, identifying strengths and opportunities for improvement based on the criteria of these agencies.
  • Development of a Roadmap
    Our experts create a strategic action plan to enhance your readiness for ESG assessments, outlining specific improvements needed to align with the standards of rating agencies.
  • Smart Solutions for High Rating Results
    We identify and focus on "smart solutions" that can rapidly boost your ESG rating, delivering a comprehensive action plan to achieve these improvements while laying the groundwork for continuous progress in ESG and sustainability.
  • Support in Agency Interactions
    We facilitate and maintain ongoing engagement with rating agencies, assisting with communications and presenting your company’s ESG initiatives and progress effectively.
  • Continuous Monitoring and Strategy Adjustment
    Following the receipt of your rating, we provide ongoing monitoring of your ESG performance, offering strategic recommendations to maintain and improve your rating, ensuring long-term alignment with evolving standards and benchmarks.
