Services
ESG Reporting and Disclosure
We offer a comprehensive range of services designed to enhance your company’s approach to ESG reporting and disclosure.

  • Content Development
    Creation of effective integrated annual reports, sustainability reports, and ESG Databooks in compliance with international standards such as GRI, ISSB-IFRS S1 S2, CSRD-ESRS, TCFD, SASB, CDP, the EU Taxonomy, and fully aligned with stakeholder expectations.
  • Improving Data Collection and Analysis Systems
    Evaluation and enhancement of your company’s current internal data collection systems and processes to improve efficiency and data accuracy.
  • Benchmarking
    Conducting thorough comparative analyses with peer companies and providing strategic recommendations to position your reporting among the leading practices.
  • Developing a Reporting Approach
    Assistance in designing robust internal and external reporting strategies, including the development of key performance indicators (KPIs) tailored to your business objectives and stakeholder expectations.
  • Supporting the Creation of an Integrated Reporting System
    Guidance in building a cohesive integrated reporting infrastructure that combines financial and non-financial aspects to ensure comprehensive ESG disclosure within your company.
