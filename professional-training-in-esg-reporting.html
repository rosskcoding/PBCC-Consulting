Services
Professional Training in ESG Reporting
Expand Your ESG Reporting Capabilities with Our Specially Designed Training Modules

  • Comprehensive ESG Reporting Workshops
    Our workshops provide in-depth training on all key standards, including GRI, TCFD, SASB, CSRD/ESRS, ISBB/IFRS, and CoP/SDG, as well as the core aspects of ESG reporting. We ensure that, after completing the training, you will be able to create reports with confidence, clarity, and in line with best industry practices.
  • GRI Standards Certification Training
    Whether you are an experienced sustainability professional or a beginner, our certified GRI Standards training will help you enhance the quality of your ESG reporting, understand, and coordinate the reporting process in compliance with GRI requirements. Upon completing our course, you will receive a globally recognised GRI certificate, confirming your expertise and recognition in the field of sustainability.
  • Reporting under ESRS (CSRD), SFDR, and the EU Taxonomy
    Through our courses on CSRD, the EU Taxonomy, ESRS, and SFDR, you will gain a thorough understanding of European regulatory requirements. We will prepare you to meet the new sustainability reporting standards within the EU framework.
  • Workshops on IFRS S1 and S2 Reporting Standards
    With the introduction of the new IFRS S1 and S2 standards by the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB), our workshops provide deep knowledge and practical skills to successfully navigate this significant change in global sustainability reporting for financial markets.
